Why you feel so bad

You live in a world that isn't built for humans. Feeling bad is the normal reaction.

Evolutionary mismatch is what happens when an animal shaped for one world has to live in another.

It is hard to see in yourself, but easy to see in other animals.

Red while the animal gets no answer, teal once it does. The cardinal gets no colour, because its study recorded only whether the bird reacted.

Nobody thinks the animals are the problem.

Humans are animals too. The same thing is happening to you.

A feeling is a prompt to act. When the act gets an answer from the world, the feeling ends. That is a closed loop.

In the tank and in the flat the answer never comes, so the feeling stays. That is an open loop.

Sometimes an answer does come, and it is a fake.

Sea turtles hatch on a beach at night, and the hatchlings have to find the sea.

The hatchling only checks where the open, bright horizon is.

The office works on a human the same way. A human recognises its own people by seeing the same faces every day and working alongside them. An office offers both.

Nobody built the office to fool you. The next fake was built on purpose.

A lure gets the strike without the fish. A thing built to do that is called a proxy.

The feed on your phone is one.

Some fakes work better than the real thing.

Offered a normal egg and a giant one, most choose the giant one. A fake that beats the real thing is called a hyperstimulus.

A real partner takes weeks. Porn takes one tap.

Food is built the same way. Food companies adjust the sugar, fat and salt until people eat as much as possible.

“I can't stop eating.”

The last kind has no fake in it. The thing is real, and there is far too much of it.

You were built to be compared with a few dozen people. Now you are compared with everyone alive.

Danger works the same way. A human can run from one danger. Nobody can run from every danger on Earth.

“I'm anxious all the time.”

The orca is shaped for the sea. A human is shaped for a world too. For nearly all of human history it looked like this.

Nobody has to go back.

That world was hard, and this one is safer. What that world had was answers.

The full list of what a human needs, with the evidence for each item, is in Cor.

A feeling that gets no answer stays on, and the days add up.

Then it gets a name: anxiety, burnout, depression. The name says the fault is in you.

Doctors, coaches and apps each sell you a different fault to fix in yourself.

Your feelings are right.

Only a clear physical defect is a disease, whether a test has found it yet or not.

A brain infection or a thyroid fault is a defect. Then you are sick, and the feeling is a symptom. A gene or a receptor that only makes you more sensitive is no defect and no cause.

Where no defect is the cause, you are not sick and there is nothing in you to fix. You are a human reacting normally to a world that isn't built for humans. Genes only set how easily you are triggered and how big the reaction is.

That holds for every label, psychosis and bipolar included. Demismatch reads psychosis without a defect as a feeling that got no answer and turned all the way up. Where a feeling gets its answer, it never has to get that far.

Where no defect is the cause, the drug prescribed for the label gets replaced by the real thing, such as people who need you and give back. The pill goes as that comes in. The real thing is hard to build while the drug dulls you, so bring the dose down while you build it. Drug adverts said the pills correct a chemical imbalance. Where no defect is the cause, the only chemical imbalance is the one the drug makes. Expect the feeling to come back as the dose drops. Doctors call that a relapse. It is the original feeling returning, plus withdrawal.

With certain drugs, and with alcohol, stopping suddenly is biologically dangerous. Never stop those suddenly, and come off them with a doctor. It doesn't have to be the doctor who put you on them. A doctor or a pharmacist can tell you whether yours is one of them. Where a defect is the cause, the drug may be treating a disease.

Here is one label from start to finish.

ADHD is a name for people who move a lot and notice everything, once school or an office puts them in a chair all day. Where there is no defect, autism and ADHD are variation, with advantages in a life that fits.

So now what

  • Ask a doctor to check for a physical cause, such as a thyroid fault.
  • See the same people in the same place every week.
  • Write down who needed you this week.
  • Show finished work to someone you know.
  • Get the same people on fixed days: a grandparent, neighbour or friend who takes the kids at the same time every week.
  • Fill the evening first. A blocked app leaves the same empty hours.
