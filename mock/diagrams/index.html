The pair as a diagram

Six ways to draw a pair. No frame, no scenery, no video: every picture is drawn in the page, in ink, with red for the bad feeling and green for the good one. Each page shows the orca pair and the human pair inside a piece of the real page. Made for a phone.

1. The loop itselfA circle and a dot that travels it. The figure is red while it feels bad and green when the answer has come. In the wrong world the circle is open where the answer should come, and the dot turns back.2. Silhouettes onlyThe animal, its family, a call that goes out and an answer that comes back. In the tank the call stops at a line.3. The signal onlyOne figure and its glow. It feels bad, it calls, the answer comes and the glow turns green. In the wrong world nothing comes back and the glow stays red.4. Words inside the drawingThe loop with every line at its place on it. No captions under the drawing. A dot shows where the story is.5. No motion at allThe same drawing as option 4, standing still. It shows whether movement adds anything.6. The loop between twoMine. What the animal does is an arrow out, the answer is an arrow back, and together they close a loop. In the wrong world the arrow out ends at a line and no arrow comes back.
