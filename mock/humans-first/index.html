Why you feel so bad

You live in a world that isn't built for humans. Feeling bad is the normal reaction.

Most people don't know this

Emotions are signals

A feeling tells you whether what is happening is good or bad for staying alive and having children. Anxiety comes with a threat and sadness with a loss. Enthusiasm comes with an opportunity and joy with a success.

Randolph Nesse, Good Reasons for Bad Feelings, 2019

This is called

Evolutionary mismatch

It is what happens when an animal shaped for one world has to live in another.

It is hard to see in yourself, but easy to see in other animals.

Nobody thinks the orca is the problem.

Six more animals

The glow is red while the animal gets no answer and teal once it does. The cardinal has no glow, because its study recorded only whether the bird reacted.

Humans are animals too. The same thing is happening to you.

People with people live longer

In 148 studies of 308,849 people, those with strong ties to other people were 50% more likely to be alive at the end, after 7.5 years on average. The effect is about as large as that of quitting smoking.

Holt-Lunstad, Smith and Layton, PLoS Medicine, 2010

A feeling is a prompt to act.

This is called

Closed loop

The act gets an answer from the world, and the feeling ends.

This is called

Open loop

In the tank and in the flat the answer never comes, so the feeling stays.

Sometimes an answer does come, and it is a fake.

This is called

Proxy

A proxy is a fake answer. Something comes back, and the feeling stays.

A human recognises its own people by seeing the same faces every day and working alongside them. An office offers both.

Nobody built the office to fool you. The next proxy was built on purpose.

Most people don't know this

Cheered up, still lonely

In one experiment, 151 adults thought and wrote for a few minutes about a real friend, a favourite media figure or a topic with no people in it. The media figure lifted a bad mood about as much as the friend did. It did not lower loneliness.

Stein, Liebers and Faiss, 2024

Anglers do the same to a pike.

A lure gets the strike without the fish.

The lure and the feed on your phone are proxies built on purpose.

Some fakes work better than the real thing.

This is called

Hyperstimulus

A hyperstimulus is a proxy that beats the real thing.

A real partner takes weeks, and porn takes one tap.

Wanting is not liking

Rats whose dopamine was taken away still liked a sweet taste and stopped going after it. Dopamine drives the wanting.

Berridge and Robinson, 1998

It works on birds too.

Food is built the same way. Food companies adjust the sugar, fat and salt until people eat as much as possible.

Ultra-processed food makes you eat more

In a trial, 20 adults ate as much as they wanted, two weeks from ultra-processed meals and two weeks from unprocessed meals. Both offered the same calories, sugar, salt and fibre. On the ultra-processed meals they ate about 500 calories a day more and gained 0.9 kg.

Hall and others, Cell Metabolism, 2019

Sometimes nothing is fake. The thing is real, and there is far too much of it.

Most people don't know this

Self-esteem measures acceptance

The sociometer hypothesis says self-esteem reads how accepted you are by other people. In five studies with students, it moved with how included or excluded people felt.

Leary and others, 1995

You were built to be compared with a few dozen people. Now you are compared with everyone alive.

Danger works the same way. A human can run from one danger, and nobody can run from every danger on Earth.

“I'm anxious all the time.”

The orca is shaped for the sea. A human is shaped for a world too. For nearly all of human history it looked like this.

This is called

EEA

EEA stands for environment of evolutionary adaptedness. It is the world a species is shaped for.

Humans raise children together

In almost all of 45 populations studied, more children survived when at least one relative besides the mother was there. The reviewers conclude that humans evolved to raise children as an extended family.

Sear and Mace, 2008

A feeling that gets no answer stays on, and the days add up.

Then it gets a name: anxiety, burnout, depression. The name says the fault is in you.

Doctors, coaches and apps each sell you a different fault to fix in yourself.

Bad feelings protect you

A cough, a fever and pain protect the body. Randolph Nesse puts anxiety, jealousy, anger and low mood in the same group. They go off when something bad is happening. They are unpleasant, and they are useful.

Randolph Nesse, Good Reasons for Bad Feelings, 2019

Your feelings are right.

Only a clear physical defect is a disease, whether a test has found it yet or not.

A brain infection or a thyroid fault is a defect. Then you are sick, and the feeling is a symptom. A gene or a receptor that only makes you more sensitive is no defect and no cause.

Where no defect is the cause, you are not sick and there is nothing in you to fix. You are a human reacting normally to a world that isn't built for humans. Genes only set how easily you are triggered and how big the reaction is.

That holds for every label, psychosis and bipolar included. Demismatch reads psychosis without a defect as a feeling that got no answer and turned all the way up. Where a feeling gets its answer, it never has to get that far.

Where no defect is the cause, the drug prescribed for the label gets replaced by the real thing, such as people who need you and give back. The real thing is hard to build while the drug dulls you, so bring the dose down while you build it. Drug adverts said the pills correct a chemical imbalance. Where no defect is the cause, the only chemical imbalance is the one the drug makes. Expect the feeling to come back as the dose drops. Doctors call that a relapse. It is the original feeling returning, plus withdrawal.

With certain drugs, and with alcohol, stopping suddenly is biologically dangerous. Never stop those suddenly, and come off them with a doctor. It doesn't have to be the doctor who put you on them. A doctor or a pharmacist can tell you whether yours is one of them. Where a defect is the cause, the drug may be treating a disease.

A child gets a label the same way.

ADHD is a name for people who move a lot and notice everything, once school or an office puts them in a chair all day. Where there is no defect, autism and ADHD are variation, with advantages in a life that fits.

So now what

  • Ask a doctor to check for a physical cause, such as a thyroid fault.
  • See the same people in the same place every week.
  • Write down who needed you this week.
  • Show finished work to someone you know.
  • Get the same people on fixed days: a grandparent, neighbour or friend who takes the kids at the same time every week.
  • Fill the evening first. A blocked app leaves the same empty hours.
