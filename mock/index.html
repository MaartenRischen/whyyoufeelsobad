Three directions

Mock-ups for whyyoufeelsobad.com, 18 November 2026. Each one is the top of the page only: the opening, the stickleback and the human child. Made to be judged on a phone. The words are the ones on the site and in the film; nothing new is written. The pictures are the film itself, drawn live, not video files.

The page takes the colour of the sky. All five subjects of the film are in this one. B. The film is inside the wordsA black screen with the sentence cut out of it, and the film showing through the letters. Scrolling pushes through one letter into the picture. C. Same words, two worldsThe sentence is said once. Under it the same animal answers twice, in two pictures that loop: the world it was built for, and the changed one. One flick moves one step.
