From part 1 to part 2

Six ways to show a pair of loops. In all of them only one loop moves at a time. Each page shows the orca pair, then the human pair. Made for a phone.

A. Text betweenPart 1 plays. Two lines under it say what you saw and turn to the wrong world. Then part 2. The loop nearest the middle of the screen plays. The other one stands still.B. Scroll starts part 2Part 2 stands still until you scroll to it. Then part 1 stops on its last picture.C. One frame, two partsPart 1, a cut card, part 2, and round again, all in one frame. The two bars on top show which part is on. Tap the picture to jump to the other part.D. Taking turnsBoth stay on screen. One plays to its end and stops, then the other plays. The yellow line shows whose turn it is.E. Step by stepOne line in the first world, then the same line in the second, then the next line. The answer and the missing answer come right after each other.F. You make the changePart 1 repeats until you press the dark button under it. Then the same frame becomes part 2.
